Work

Building a channel the store had never worked.

Food service franchise, United States — catering · July 2026 — ongoing

Two years of order history showed the same pattern every month. The store competed hard in the channels it already served and had never touched the one beside it. Thirteen school addresses in the file had produced $180 in two years. There was no list, no pricing, and no contacts — not from the current operator, and not from the previous ownership.

We built the segment from nothing: cold email, one segment at a time, with the offer rebuilt for each.

  • 11 days from first email to the first signed vendor account
  • $1,055 single order — the largest in the store's two-year record, in any channel
  • 8% reply rate across 165 cold emails in six weeks
  • $180 what the same segment had produced in the two years before

A further $12,000 in catering is under negotiation. It is listed separately because it is pipeline, not result.

Most local businesses compete in the channels they already serve. The work was in the one this store had never opened.

Practice

Ingold is a consultancy, not an agency. We are responsible for decisions — where a brand stands, what it does next, what the numbers actually say — and not for the hands that carry them out. Production is arranged separately, with people we choose and direct.

Most practices of this size stop at judgement. We also count. Market data, competitive sets, and customer behaviour are gathered and analysed with technical tools rather than estimated, which is the difference between an opinion about a market and a measurement of one.

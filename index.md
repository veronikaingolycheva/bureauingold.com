## Approach

**Depth over volume.** A small number of companies at a time. Selection is the method, not a stage we intend to grow out of.

**Position before promotion.** Positioning is not messaging. Messaging is what a brand says; positioning is the place it occupies when nobody is saying anything. The second is settled before money is spent on the first.

**Built to compound.** A campaign ends. A position, a measured result, and a team able to work without us do not.

## Services

**Positioning.** Who the customer is, what the brand stands against, and the one sentence an outsider repeats correctly. Everything downstream rests on it.

**Diagnostics and analytics.** The current picture in numbers. Where margin leaks, which channels carry the business, what a launch cost to acquire, and which of it will repeat.

**Growth strategy.** Channel architecture, funnel economics, budget allocation, and the metrics decisions are made on — written as a plan a team can run for six to twelve months.

**Creative direction.** The visual and verbal language of the brand: how it looks, what it says, and what it declines to say. Set here, executed by others.

Engagements are scoped individually. Fees on request.

**Not in the practice:** social media management, content production, media buying, site maintenance, reporting as a standing service. We are responsible for decisions, not for hands — and we will name people who do this work well.

## Work

### Building a channel the store had never worked

Food service franchise, United States — catering. July 2026 — ongoing.

Two years of order history showed the same pattern every month. The store competed hard in the channels it already served and had never touched the one beside it. Thirteen school addresses in the file had produced $180 in two years. There was no list, no pricing, and no contacts — not from the current operator, and not from the previous ownership.

We built the segment from nothing: cold email, one segment at a time, with the offer rebuilt for each.

| Figure | What it is |
|---|---|
| 11 days | from first email to the first signed vendor account |
| $1,055 | single order — the largest in the store's two-year record, in any channel |
| 8% | reply rate across 165 cold emails in six weeks |
| $180 | what the same segment had produced in the two years before |

A further $12,000 in catering is under negotiation. It is listed separately because it is pipeline, not result.

Most local businesses compete in the channels they already serve. The work was in the one this store had never opened.

## Practice

Bureau Ingold is a consultancy, not an agency. We are responsible for decisions — where a brand stands, what it does next, what the numbers actually say — and not for the hands that carry them out. Production is arranged separately, with people we choose and direct.
